Our philosophy

The parent consultation model

The parent consultation model of ABA services is built on the belief that parents know their child best, while also recognizing the importance of the clinical expertise and guidance provided by a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA). This collaborative approach combines the parent’s deep understanding of their child’s personality, strengths, and daily life with the BCBA’s specialized training in behavior, learning, development, and evidence-based intervention strategies.

Rather than therapy happening only during scheduled sessions, a parent consultation model empowers families to create meaningful progress within everyday routines and natural environments. The BCBA provides individualized assessment, coaching, problem-solving, and practical strategies — while supporting parents in implementing interventions in ways that feel realistic, compassionate, and sustainable for their family.

Our goal is not to replace parental intuition, but to strengthen and support it through professional guidance, education, and collaboration. We believe the most meaningful and lasting progress happens when families feel confident, supported, and equipped with both expert knowledge and practical tools.
