Insurance & private pay

Flexible options for your family.

Every family’s situation is different. We’ll help you understand what’s possible — just reach out and we’ll walk through it with you.

Insurance

Coverage for ABA and parent-consultation services can vary widely from plan to plan. Because the details depend on your specific insurer, policy, and benefits, the best next step is simply to get in touch — we’re happy to talk through your situation and help you understand your options.

Have a question about insurance or coverage? Email us at info@villagebehavioralservices.com and we’ll help you figure out the right path forward.

Private pay

We also welcome private-pay families. Private pay can be a straightforward, flexible way to begin services on a timeline that works for you, without waiting on insurance authorization. Reach out and we’ll share details on what private-pay services look like.

  • We work with a range of payment situations — insurance and private pay.
  • No-pressure conversation to understand your options before you commit.
  • Clear, family-friendly guidance every step of the way.

Not sure where to start?

Send us a note and we’ll help you understand your insurance and private-pay options.
