Free, on-demand parenting support — powered by AI and backed by Board Certified Behavior Analyst oversight. Real guidance whenever you need it, day or night.

Guidance that doesn’t wait for your next appointment

Parenting questions don’t keep to a schedule — and you shouldn’t have to wait until your next consultation to get help. Modern Village gives families a free, on-demand companion for the in-between moments: the tough mornings, the unexpected meltdowns, the “what do I do right now?” questions.

It’s powered by AI so it’s available instantly, anytime — and it’s built with BCBA oversight, so the strategies it offers are grounded in the same evidence-based, compassionate approach we bring to our consultations.

It’s free. Modern Village is available to families at no cost — a natural companion to the personalized guidance you get through parent consultation.
