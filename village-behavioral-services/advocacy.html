Educational advocacy

A knowledgeable ally in your child’s corner.

Our team has years of experience inside public schools. We know how the system works — and we’re well equipped to help you navigate it with confidence.

Guidance you can lean on

Navigating special education can feel overwhelming. Because our team has spent years working within public schools, we understand the process from the inside — the meetings, the paperwork, the timelines, and the rights your family has along the way. We help you prepare, ask the right questions, and advocate effectively for the support your child deserves.

How we support families

  • IEP support — preparing for meetings, reviewing goals, and helping you understand and participate in the process.
  • 504 plans — guidance on eligibility, accommodations, and implementation.
  • Other accommodations — help identifying and requesting supports that fit your child’s needs.
  • Independent Educational Evaluations (IEEs) — if you disagree with an evaluation done by your school district and would like a second opinion, we provide IEEs.

Disagree with your district’s evaluation? You have the right to seek an Independent Educational Evaluation. We provide IEEs to give your family a thorough, objective second opinion you can bring back to the table.
