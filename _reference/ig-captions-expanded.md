### POST 3 — Strategy Card (iPad Transition)

📱➡️🍽️ The iPad-to-dinner battle, solved.

Here's the 3-step ABA strategy our AI Coach gives parents for screen transitions:

1️⃣ Give a warning — "2 more minutes, then iPad goes night-night!"
2️⃣ Offer a choice — "Do you want to help set the table or pick your seat?"
3️⃣ Follow through calmly — Remove iPad. No negotiating. Stay neutral.

⭐ When they transition: "You did it! That was so grown-up!"

The key? Consistency. Do this the same way every time and watch it click.

### POST 7 — Save This (5 De-escalation Phrases)

📌 SAVE THIS for the next meltdown.

5 phrases that actually de-escalate:

1. "I'm right here with you."
2. "I can see this is really hard."
3. "Do you need a hug or space?"
4. "Let's take 3 deep breaths together."
5. "You're safe. I'm not going anywhere."

Notice what's missing? No "calm down." No "stop crying." No "you're fine."

Those dismiss the feeling. These validate it.

Validation first. Strategy second. Every time.
