Post 11 — Sensory Tip
Modern Village
Know the Difference
Sensory-seeking vs. sensory-avoiding
Seeking 🔊
Crashes into furniture
Craves tight hugs
Mouths objects constantly
Avoiding 🤞
Covers ears at normal noise
Refuses certain textures
Avoids being touched
Your child may be both — in different situations. Neither is bad behavior. Both need a response, not a reaction.

Post 19 — Trigger Chart
Modern Village
The 4 Functions of Behavior
Why is my child doing this?
🎯
Tangible
To get something they want — a toy, snack, or activity
Try: offer choices
🚪
Escape
To avoid or get out of a demand, task, or situation
Try: first-then + breaks
👀
Attention
To get a reaction from a person — even a negative one
Try: planned ignoring
🔊
Sensory
To regulate internal sensory needs — calming or stimulating
Try: sensory diet

Post 23 — Crisis Tip
Modern Village
Meltdown Protocol
What to do in the first 60 seconds
1
Stop talking
The brain can't reason during dysregulation. Reduce language to 2–3 words max: "I'm here. You're safe."
2
Ensure safety
Remove objects that can hurt. Create space. Lower your own body to their level if possible.
3
Co-regulate first
Model calm breathing. Your nervous system helps regulate theirs. Stay present — even silent presence helps.
Problem-solving comes after. Not during.
