# POOLING AGREEMENT

**Between Jorrel Patterson and Ariana Roberts**

**Effective Date:** [DATE]

---

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, Jorrel Patterson ("Jorrel") owns one hundred percent (100%) of the membership interests of Modern Village Services LLC, a California limited liability company (the "MSO");

WHEREAS, Ariana Roberts ("Ariana") is the sole member of Modern Village LLC, a California limited liability company already formed and owned by Ariana (the "Clinical LLC");

WHEREAS, the MSO and the Clinical LLC are party to a Management Services Agreement under which the MSO provides non-clinical management services to the Clinical LLC;

WHEREAS, because the clinical practice is conducted through the Clinical LLC — which the parties have elected to restrict to ownership by a duly licensed BCBA in order to preserve clinical integrity — Ariana must hold the Clinical LLC in her individual capacity, and therefore a pooling of economic interests across both entities must be achieved by a contractual agreement between the individual principals rather than through a single ownership structure;

WHEREAS, Jorrel and Ariana desire to share the combined economic value created by the MSO and the Clinical LLC upon a sale, in accordance with the terms of the Modern Village Partnership Term Sheet dated 2026-04-14 (the "Term Sheet"); and

WHEREAS, this Agreement reflects the parties' intent to split the net combined proceeds of any Liquidity Event on a 50/50 basis, subject to (i) recovery of Jorrel's documented capital and expenses, (ii) Ariana's monthly vesting, and (iii) good-leaver and bad-leaver provisions;

WHEREAS, the parties acknowledge that while California's Moscone-Knox Professional Corporation Act may not strictly require a Professional Corporation for BCBA practices, the parties have elected to use a California limited liability company with clinical-practice restrictions to maintain clinical integrity and to prepare for potential regulatory changes.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein, the parties agree as follows:

---

## ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Definitions.**

- **"Actively Contributing"** means, with respect to Ariana, that on a given date ALL of the following are true: (a) she is employed by the Clinical LLC as Chief Clinical Officer or in a comparable senior clinical role; (b) she is providing a minimum of ten (10) hours per week of bona fide services to the Clinical LLC; (c) she has not given notice of resignation (and such notice has not been rescinded by mutual written agreement); and (d) she has not been terminated by the Clinical LLC for Cause.

- **"Bad Leaver Event"** has the meaning set forth in Section 5.2.

- **"Capital Recovery"** means the amount to which Jorrel is entitled under Section 3.3.

- **"Cause"** has the meaning set forth in Section 5.3.

- **"Combined Sale Proceeds"** means the aggregate gross consideration actually received by Jorrel, Ariana, the MSO, the Clinical LLC, and/or their respective equityholders in connection with a Liquidity Event (including any cash, securities, earn-out payments, escrow releases, and assumption of debt), less (a) transaction expenses (legal, banker, accounting, escrow, and similar), (b) taxes payable by the selling entity as a direct result of the transaction (excluding personal income tax of the parties), and (c) amounts required to satisfy senior indebtedness of the MSO or Clinical LLC.

- **"Effective Date"** means the date on which this Agreement is effective upon execution by all parties whose signature is required below.

- **"Good Leaver Event"** has the meaning set forth in Section 5.1.

- **"Investor Equity"** means the portion of the Combined Sale Proceeds allocable to any bona fide third-party investor in the MSO who holds equity at the time of the Liquidity Event.

- **"Liquidity Event"** means any of the following: (a) the sale of all or substantially all of the assets of the MSO, the Clinical LLC, or both; (b) a merger or consolidation of the MSO, the Clinical LLC, or both with or into another entity in which the pre-transaction equityholders do not retain majority voting control; (c) a change of control of the MSO, the Clinical LLC, or both; or (d) a dissolution and distribution of assets of the MSO, the Clinical LLC, or both resulting in cash or property distributable to equityholders.

- **"MSO"** means Modern Village Services LLC.

- **"Net Poolable Proceeds"** means the Combined Sale Proceeds minus (a) Investor Equity and (b) Capital Recovery.

- **"Clinical LLC"** means Modern Village LLC, a California limited liability company already formed and wholly owned by Ariana Roberts.

- **"Reserved Matters"** has the meaning set forth in Section 7.2 of the Term Sheet and Section 6 of this Agreement.

- **"Term Sheet"** means the Modern Village Partnership Term Sheet dated 2026-04-14.

- **"Vesting Percentage"** has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

## ARTICLE 2 — PURPOSE AND STRUCTURE

**2.1 Purpose.** The purpose of this Agreement is to establish a binding contractual mechanism between Jorrel and Ariana, in their individual capacities, to share the net combined value of the MSO and the Clinical LLC upon a Liquidity Event.

**2.2 Not a Partnership.** Nothing in this Agreement is intended to create a partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship. The MSO and the Clinical LLC shall remain independent entities. Jorrel and Ariana are parties to this Agreement solely in their individual capacities.

**2.3 Individual Obligations.** Each party's obligations under this Agreement are personal to such party and binding upon such party's heirs, executors, administrators, successors, and permitted assigns.

---

## ARTICLE 3 — POOLING OF PROCEEDS UPON A LIQUIDITY EVENT

**3.1 Pooling Trigger.** Upon the occurrence of a Liquidity Event, Jorrel and Ariana shall compute and distribute the Combined Sale Proceeds in accordance with this Article 3.

**3.2 Order of Distribution.** The Combined Sale Proceeds shall be distributed in the following order of priority:

(a) **First**, to any investor who holds Investor Equity in the MSO, in accordance with the terms of such investor's governing documents;

(b) **Second**, to Jorrel as Capital Recovery in accordance with Section 3.3;

(c) **Third**, the remaining amount (the "Net Poolable Proceeds") shall be split fifty percent (50%) to each of Jorrel and Ariana, subject to adjustment for Ariana's Vesting Percentage pursuant to Article 4 and Section 5.

**3.3 Capital Recovery.**

(a) **Eligible Items.** Capital Recovery shall consist of documented amounts advanced or spent by Jorrel, directly or indirectly, in support of the MSO or the combined Modern Village business, including without limitation:

(i) cash marketing spend (ads, content, CRM tools, scraper tooling, industry events, sponsorships);

(ii) technology infrastructure costs (hosting, APIs, software licenses, developer tools);

(iii) outside contractors and service providers (development, design, legal, accounting, consulting);

(iv) legal and accounting fees attributable to the MSO and/or the partnership structure;

(v) personal capital contributions by Jorrel to the MSO; and

(vi) such other bona fide out-of-pocket expenditures as the parties mutually agree to in writing.

(b) **Excluded Items.** The following shall NOT be eligible for Capital Recovery: (i) Jorrel's salary, draws, or distributions from the MSO; (ii) the imputed value of Jorrel's personal time; (iii) expenses reimbursed from any other source; and (iv) expenses not supported by documentation.

(c) **Cap.** Capital Recovery shall not exceed the lesser of (i) the actual documented total, (ii) twenty percent (20%) of Combined Sale Proceeds, or (iii) five hundred thousand dollars ($500,000).

(d) **Documentation.** Every item claimed as Capital Recovery must be supported by invoices, receipts, bank records, or equivalent documentation. Items lacking such documentation shall be excluded.

(e) **Quarterly Ledger.** The MSO shall maintain a running Capital Recovery Ledger listing each item, the date, amount, category, and source document, and shall deliver a copy of the Ledger to Ariana no less frequently than once each calendar quarter. Ariana shall have thirty (30) days to object in writing to any item; unobjected items shall be deemed accepted.

(f) **Dispute.** A good-faith dispute over any specific item shall not delay a Liquidity Event but shall be resolved per Article 10.

**3.4 Pooling Mechanic — Worked Example.** By way of illustration only, and consistent with Section 3.5 of the Term Sheet:

- Combined Sale Proceeds: $10,000,000 ($9M MSO + $1M Clinical LLC)
- Investor Equity: $1,800,000
- Subtotal: $8,200,000
- Capital Recovery to Jorrel: $300,000
- Net Poolable Proceeds: $7,900,000
- 50/50 split (assuming Ariana 100% vested): $3,950,000 each
- Jorrel total: $3,950,000 + $300,000 = **$4,250,000**
- Ariana total: **$3,950,000**

The example is for illustration only and is not a representation of actual future proceeds.

## ARTICLE 4 — ARIANA'S VESTING

**4.1 Vesting Schedule.** Ariana's fifty percent (50%) share of Net Poolable Proceeds under Section 3.2(c) shall vest as follows:

(a) Vesting begins on the Effective Date.

(b) Vesting accrues monthly in increments of one forty-eighth (1/48) of the full interest per completed month of continuous Actively Contributing service.

(c) There is no cliff. The first increment vests at the end of the first completed month.

(d) Full vesting is achieved at the end of the forty-eighth (48th) month.

(e) "Vesting Percentage" at any given time means the cumulative vested percentage of the full interest as of such time.

**4.2 Acceleration Upon Liquidity Event.** If, on the closing date of a Liquidity Event, Ariana is Actively Contributing, Ariana's Vesting Percentage shall automatically be deemed one hundred percent (100%) for purposes of that Liquidity Event, regardless of elapsed time.

**4.3 Effect of Non-Actively-Contributing Status.** If, as of the closing date of a Liquidity Event, Ariana is NOT Actively Contributing (due to a prior Good Leaver Event), Ariana's Vesting Percentage shall equal the Vesting Percentage as of the date Ariana ceased being Actively Contributing.

**4.4 Forfeiture of Unvested Amounts.** Any portion of Ariana's 50% share that is not vested as of the Liquidity Event closing date shall revert to Jorrel's side of the pool (i.e., added to Jorrel's distribution) and shall not be paid to Ariana.

## ARTICLE 5 — GOOD LEAVER AND BAD LEAVER PROVISIONS

**5.1 Good Leaver Events.** A "Good Leaver Event" means any of the following:

(a) termination of Ariana's service by the Clinical LLC without Cause;

(b) death of Ariana;

(c) permanent disability of Ariana, defined as inability to perform her duties for six (6) or more consecutive months (as reasonably determined in accordance with the Buy-Sell Agreement);

(d) mutual separation agreed in writing between Ariana and Jorrel; or

(e) voluntary resignation by Ariana to pursue an unrelated opportunity that is not a direct competitor of Modern Village.

**5.2 Good Leaver Consequences.** Upon a Good Leaver Event:

(a) Ariana's vesting immediately stops and her Vesting Percentage is locked as of the date of the Good Leaver Event;

(b) Ariana retains the right to receive her vested portion of Net Poolable Proceeds upon a future Liquidity Event; and

(c) any unvested portion reverts to Jorrel's side of the pool.

**5.3 Bad Leaver Events.** A "Bad Leaver Event" means any of the following:

(a) termination of Ariana's service by the Clinical LLC for Cause;

(b) material breach of the non-solicit or non-competition covenants in Section 8 of the Term Sheet that is not cured within the applicable cure period; or

(c) voluntary resignation by Ariana to join, form, or actively support a direct competitor of Modern Village within two (2) years after resignation.

For purposes of this Agreement, "Cause" means: theft, fraud, willful misconduct, conviction of (or plea of no contest to) a crime involving moral turpitude, gross negligence in clinical duties, or material breach of this Agreement, the Clinical LLC Operating Agreement, the MSA, or the Buy-Sell Agreement that remains uncured for thirty (30) days after written notice.

**5.4 Bad Leaver Consequences.** Upon a Bad Leaver Event:

(a) Ariana forfeits ALL vested and unvested rights under this Agreement;

(b) the forfeited rights revert to Jorrel's side of the pool in their entirety; and

(c) Ariana shall have no claim against Jorrel, the MSO, or the Clinical LLC for any portion of Net Poolable Proceeds on any future Liquidity Event (subject only to the Buy-Sell Agreement for valuation of the Clinical LLC entity itself).

**5.5 Procedural Protections.** A determination of a Bad Leaver Event shall be made in good faith by Jorrel with at least fifteen (15) days' prior written notice to Ariana identifying the basis. Ariana shall have the right to contest the determination through the dispute resolution procedures of Article 10.

## ARTICLE 6 — RESERVED MATTERS

**6.1 Reserved Matters.** Consistent with Section 7.2 of the Term Sheet, and notwithstanding that the MSO and the Clinical LLC are separately owned, Jorrel covenants that he will not cause or permit the MSO to take, and Ariana covenants that she will not cause or permit the Clinical LLC to take, any of the following actions without the prior written consent of the other party:

(a) sale of the MSO, the Clinical LLC, or substantially all assets of either;

(b) dissolution of the MSO or the Clinical LLC;

(c) incurrence by the MSO of debt exceeding $100,000 in the aggregate;

(d) issuance of new equity in the MSO to any third party;

(e) amendment of this Agreement or the MSA Management Fee structure;

(f) entry into a new line of business outside behavioral health support; and

(g) any other action identified elsewhere in this Agreement as a Reserved Matter.

**6.2 Remedy for Breach.** A breach of Section 6.1 shall entitle the non-breaching party to injunctive relief in addition to monetary damages, given the unique nature of the economic interests at stake.

## ARTICLE 9 — TERM AND TERMINATION

**9.1 Term.** This Agreement shall remain in effect until the first to occur of:

(a) a Liquidity Event, upon which the parties' rights under this Agreement shall be computed, distributed, and fully discharged;

(b) the mutual written termination of this Agreement by both parties; or

(c) the dissolution of both the MSO and the Clinical LLC without a Liquidity Event, in which case this Agreement shall terminate with no further obligations.

**9.2 Surviving Provisions.** Articles 6, 7, 10, and 11 shall survive termination.

## ARTICLE 10 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**10.1 Informal Resolution.** Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall first be discussed between the parties in good faith for a period of thirty (30) days.

**10.2 Mediation.** If informal resolution fails, the parties shall engage a mutually agreeable mediator for up to sixty (60) days of non-binding mediation.

**10.3 Binding Arbitration.** If mediation fails, the dispute shall be resolved by final, binding arbitration in Los Angeles County, California under the rules of JAMS. The prevailing party shall be entitled to reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.

**10.4 Injunctive Relief.** Notwithstanding the foregoing, either party may seek preliminary injunctive relief from a court of competent jurisdiction to enforce Article 6 or to preserve the status quo pending arbitration.
