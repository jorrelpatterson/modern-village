# COMMISSION AND BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT

**Between**
**Ariana Roberts** ("Ariana")
**and**
**Modern Village Services LLC** (the "MSO")

**Effective Date:** [DATE]

---

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, Ariana serves as Chief Clinical Officer and Sole Manager of Modern Village LLC (the "Clinical LLC") and, beyond her clinical role, contributes to the Modern Village business through business development, partner outreach, and capital-raising activities;

WHEREAS, the MSO and Ariana desire to formalize the compensation Ariana will earn for such business development contributions, separate from her clinical income through the Clinical LLC, as contemplated by Section 6 of the Modern Village Partnership Term Sheet dated 2026-04-14 (the "Term Sheet");

WHEREAS, the parties intend that commissions paid under this Agreement be paid to Ariana in her capacity as a principal and officer of the Modern Village business — not as an unaffiliated third-party broker or finder — so as to avoid unregistered broker-dealer concerns; and

WHEREAS, the parties intend that this Agreement be read in harmony with the Pooling Agreement, the MSA, and the other Modern Village contracts.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein, the parties agree as follows:

---

## ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.**

- **"Actively Contributing"** has the meaning set forth in the Pooling Agreement.
- **"Attribution Requirements"** has the meaning set forth in Section 3.1.
- **"Bad Leaver"** has the meaning set forth in the Pooling Agreement.
- **"Commission"** means any commission, finder's fee, or origination payment earned under Article 2.
- **"Effective Date"** means the date on which this Agreement is effective upon execution by all parties whose signature is required below.
- **"Qualified Contract"** means a contract that meets the attribution and category requirements of this Agreement and has actually closed and begun generating revenue or funding.
- **"Tail Period"** means the post-departure period during which earned Commissions continue to be paid under Section 6.
- **"Term Sheet"** means the Modern Village Partnership Term Sheet dated 2026-04-14.

---

## ARTICLE 2 — COMMISSION CATEGORIES

**2.1 School District Contracts.** Ariana shall be entitled to a Commission equal to **ten percent (10%) of the gross revenue** actually collected by the MSO (or the Clinical LLC, as applicable) from a school district contract Ariana sources and closes, for a period of **twenty-four (24) months** beginning on the contract start date. No Commission is payable on revenue collected after the end of the 24-month period, regardless of contract length.

**2.2 Regional Center Contracts.** Ariana shall be entitled to a Commission equal to **ten percent (10%) of the gross revenue** actually collected from a Regional Center contract Ariana sources and closes, for a period of **twenty-four (24) months** beginning on the contract start date, on the same terms as Section 2.1.

**2.3 Corporate ABA Partnerships.** Ariana shall be entitled to a Commission equal to **fifteen percent (15%) of the first-year contract value** (paid one time, not recurring) for each corporate ABA partnership (including but not limited to partnerships with Learn Behavioral, BlueSprig, or similar established behavioral health companies) that Ariana sources and closes. The Commission shall be paid within thirty (30) days after the partnership contract is signed.

**2.4 Marketplace Provider Recruits.** Ariana shall be entitled to a Commission equal to **five percent (5%) of the marketplace fees** generated by each provider Ariana personally recruits to the Modern Village marketplace, for a period of **twelve (12) months** beginning on the date the provider becomes active on the platform. Recruitment must be active and direct — mere passive referrals or social mentions do not qualify.

**2.5 Investor Sourcing.** Ariana shall be entitled to a finder's fee equal to **three percent (3%) of the invested capital**, capped at **fifty thousand dollars ($50,000) per round**, as a one-time payment for any investor Ariana actively sources and introduces to the MSO and who ultimately invests. The fee shall be paid within thirty (30) days after the round closes and funds are received.

**2.6 Exclusive Categories.** The categories listed in this Article 2 are exclusive; no Commission is payable on any other category of revenue or activity without a separate written agreement.

---

## ARTICLE 3 — ATTRIBUTION AND EXCLUSIONS

**3.1 Attribution Requirements.** To earn a Commission, Ariana must satisfy all of the following "Attribution Requirements":

(a) Ariana initiated the contact with the lead (i.e., Ariana was the first person on the Modern Village side to engage the prospective counterparty or was the first to introduce the prospect to Modern Village);

(b) Ariana was present and participated substantively at the first substantive meeting with the prospect;

(c) Ariana remained actively involved through the close of the deal (including reasonable participation in negotiations, diligence calls, and contract discussions); and

(d) Ariana documented the attribution in writing (e-mail, CRM entry, or equivalent record) within thirty (30) days after the initial introduction.

**3.2 Disputes Default to No Commission.** If attribution is disputed and Ariana cannot produce documentation satisfying Section 3.1, the default outcome is that no Commission is payable.

**3.3 Exclusions.** The following shall NOT give rise to a Commission:

(a) leads sourced through the MSO's marketing pipeline, paid advertising, SEO, scrapers, automated outreach, or other systems not initiated by Ariana;

(b) passive mentions, social-media posts, or casual referrals;

(c) retroactive claims for deals already in progress as of the Effective Date without Ariana's prior active involvement;

(d) deals that do not actually close or for which revenue is never collected;

(e) deals for which Ariana's involvement ended before the closing (except to the extent such departure qualifies for Tail Period treatment under Section 6); and

(f) any activity that, if compensated, would require a broker-dealer registration that Ariana does not hold.

**3.4 Investment Decisions.** Ariana may source investment opportunities, but all decisions regarding whether to accept, the valuation, the terms, and the closing of any investment remain exclusively with Jorrel as Managing Member of the MSO. No Commission shall be payable on any investment opportunity that is declined or on any deal for which Ariana's involvement ended before closing.

---

## ARTICLE 4 — SECURITIES LAW AND COMPLIANCE

**4.1 Principal / Officer Capacity.** The parties acknowledge that the finder's fee in Section 2.5 is paid to Ariana in her capacity as a principal, officer, and substantial contributor to the Modern Village business (via her role at the Clinical LLC and her economic interest under the Pooling Agreement), and not as an unaffiliated broker or finder. The parties intend that this structure avoid characterization of Ariana as an unregistered broker-dealer under Section 15 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 or the California Corporate Securities Law.

**4.2 No Public Solicitation.** Ariana shall not engage in general solicitation or general advertising of any MSO securities. All introductions shall be made to investors with whom Ariana has a pre-existing substantive relationship or who are reasonably believed to be accredited.

**4.3 Compliance Review.** Before any investor-sourcing Commission is paid, the MSO may, at its option, obtain a legal opinion or compliance memo confirming that the payment does not violate applicable securities laws. If compliance counsel advises against the payment structure, the parties shall negotiate in good faith to restructure the commission in a compliant manner or to forego it.

**4.4 Anti-Kickback Compliance.** No Commission shall be paid for referrals of patients, clients, or clinical services. Commissions under this Agreement are paid solely for business development activities and are not intended to compensate Ariana for generating or rewarding patient referrals. The parties will review each Commission for compliance with federal and California anti-kickback and fee-splitting laws.

---

## ARTICLE 5 — PAYMENT TERMS

**5.1 Monthly Calculation.** For recurring Commissions (Sections 2.1, 2.2, and 2.4), the MSO shall calculate earned Commissions on a monthly basis and deliver a statement to Ariana within fifteen (15) business days after the end of each month.

**5.2 Payment Timing.** Earned Commissions shall be paid within thirty (30) days after revenue is actually collected from the underlying customer or funds are actually received from the investor. Commissions are not payable on accrued but uncollected amounts.

**5.3 Chargebacks.** If a customer refunds, disputes, or otherwise reverses a payment on which a Commission was paid, or if a contract terminates early and the customer is entitled to a refund of prepaid amounts, the MSO may offset such chargeback against future Commissions or, if insufficient future Commissions exist, may demand repayment of the prior Commission within thirty (30) days.

**5.4 Withholding and Taxes.** Commissions shall be reported in accordance with applicable tax law. The parties acknowledge that Ariana is not an employee of the MSO by virtue of this Agreement, and Commissions shall be reported on IRS Form 1099 unless the parties otherwise agree in writing.

**5.5 Right to Audit.** Ariana shall have the right, upon reasonable prior notice and not more than once per calendar year, to audit the MSO's books and records relating to Commission calculations. The audit shall be at Ariana's expense unless the audit uncovers an underpayment of more than five percent (5%) of amounts owed, in which case the MSO shall reimburse Ariana's reasonable audit costs.

---

## ARTICLE 6 — TAIL PERIOD AND DEPARTURE

**6.1 Tail Period.** Subject to Section 6.2, Commissions earned under this Agreement shall continue to be paid for the stated duration (e.g., 24 months for district contracts) even after Ariana ceases to be Actively Contributing, provided that the Attribution Requirements were satisfied at the time of the original deal.

**6.2 Bad Leaver Forfeiture.** Notwithstanding Section 6.1, if Ariana becomes a Bad Leaver (as defined in the Pooling Agreement), ALL unpaid Commissions (earned or unearned) under this Agreement shall be forfeited, and the MSO shall have no obligation to make any further payments hereunder.

**6.3 Good Leaver Treatment.** If Ariana ceases to be Actively Contributing under a Good Leaver Event (as defined in the Pooling Agreement), she retains her right to Commissions earned prior to the Good Leaver Event and to the ongoing Tail Period payments for existing Qualified Contracts, but is not eligible for any new Commissions for deals closing after the Good Leaver Event.

---

## ARTICLE 7 — TERM AND TERMINATION

**7.1 Term.** This Agreement is effective on the Effective Date and continues until the earlier of:

(a) Ariana ceasing to hold her role as Chief Clinical Officer of the Clinical LLC and the expiration of the Tail Period for all Qualified Contracts;

(b) mutual written termination by the parties; or

(c) a Liquidity Event, upon which all amounts owed under this Agreement shall be paid in full or assumed by the acquirer.

**7.2 Effect of Termination.** Termination does not affect Commissions earned prior to termination for Qualified Contracts, subject to Section 6.2 (Bad Leaver forfeiture).

---

## ARTICLE 8 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

**8.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by California law, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

**8.2 Dispute Resolution.** Disputes arising out of this Agreement shall be resolved in accordance with the dispute resolution provisions of the Pooling Agreement.

**8.3 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with the Pooling Agreement, the MSA, and the other Modern Village contracts, constitutes the entire agreement of the parties regarding its subject matter.

**8.4 Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

**8.5 Severability.** If any provision is held invalid, the remaining provisions shall remain in effect.

**8.6 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, except that the MSO may assign this Agreement to a successor in connection with a Liquidity Event.

**8.7 No Employment Relationship.** Nothing in this Agreement creates an employer-employee relationship between the MSO and Ariana. Ariana's employment relationship, if any, is with the Clinical LLC.

**8.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
