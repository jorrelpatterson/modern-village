## RECITALS

WHEREAS, Modern Village Services LLC (the "Company") was organized as a limited liability company under the California Revised Uniform Limited Liability Company Act (Corporations Code § 17701.01 et seq.) by the filing of Articles of Organization (Form LLC-1) with the California Secretary of State on [FILING DATE];

WHEREAS, Jorrel Patterson ("Jorrel" or the "Member") is the sole member of the Company and desires to set forth the terms governing the ownership, management, and operation of the Company;

WHEREAS, the Company has been formed to own and operate all technology, platform, brand, intellectual property, marketing, business development, and non-clinical operations of the Modern Village business (the "Business"); and

WHEREAS, the Company is entering into a Management Services Agreement with Modern Village LLC (the "Clinical LLC"), a related California limited liability company owned by a duly licensed Board Certified Behavior Analyst, to deliver non-clinical management services to the Clinical LLC in accordance with California law.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Member adopts the following as the Operating Agreement of the Company:

## ARTICLE 5 — ALLOCATIONS AND DISTRIBUTIONS

**5.1 Allocations.** Because the Company has a single Member, all items of income, gain, loss, deduction, and credit shall be allocated entirely to the Member for federal, state, and local income tax purposes. The Company shall by default be treated as a disregarded entity for federal income tax purposes unless the Member elects otherwise.

**5.2 Distributions.** Distributions shall be made to the Member at such times and in such amounts as the Managing Member determines in his sole discretion, subject to the requirements of the Act and the Company's obligations to creditors.

**5.3 Tax Distributions.** If the Company elects to be taxed as a partnership or corporation in the future, the Managing Member may cause the Company to make tax distributions sufficient to cover the Member's tax liability arising from Company operations.

**5.4 No Distributions Impairing Solvency.** No Distribution shall be made in violation of Corporations Code § 17704.05 or any other provision of the Act.

## ARTICLE 6 — MANAGEMENT

**6.1 Management by Managing Member.** The Company is a manager-managed limited liability company. Jorrel Patterson is hereby designated as the initial Managing Member and shall serve until his resignation, death, disability, or removal by the Member.

**6.2 Authority of Managing Member.** Consistent with Section 7.1 of the Term Sheet, the Managing Member shall have full, exclusive, and unilateral authority to manage and control the business and affairs of the Company, including without limitation the authority to:

(a) set product, technology, and engineering direction;

(b) determine marketing strategy and spend;

(c) hire, fire, supervise, and set compensation for Company employees and contractors;

(d) negotiate, execute, and perform contracts, leases, and vendor relationships under fifty thousand dollars ($50,000) in aggregate annual value without further authorization;

(e) open and maintain bank and brokerage accounts;

(f) acquire, hold, and dispose of Company property;

(g) retain attorneys, accountants, and other professionals;

(h) file tax returns and make tax elections; and

(i) take any other action the Managing Member deems necessary or appropriate for the conduct of Company business.

**6.3 Reserved Matters.** Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Managing Member acknowledges that under the Pooling Agreement with Ariana Roberts, the following "Reserved Matters" require the prior written consent of Ariana Roberts as a contractual matter (although Ariana Roberts is not a Member of the Company):

(a) sale of the Company, the Clinical LLC, or substantially all of the assets of either;

(b) dissolution of the Company or the Clinical LLC;

(c) incurrence of debt by the Company exceeding one hundred thousand dollars ($100,000) in the aggregate;

(d) issuance of new equity in the Company to any third party;

(e) amendment of the Pooling Agreement or the MSA Management Fee structure;

(f) entering a new line of business outside behavioral health support; and

(g) any other matter designated as a "Reserved Matter" in the Pooling Agreement.

The failure to obtain such consent shall be a breach of the Pooling Agreement and does not independently affect the Company's corporate authority to act.

**6.4 Officers.** The Managing Member may appoint officers of the Company, including a President, Chief Operating Officer, Chief Financial Officer, Secretary, and such other officers as he determines. The initial officers are:

- **President and Chief Operating Officer:** Jorrel Patterson

**6.5 Standard of Care.** The Managing Member shall discharge his duties in good faith, with the care an ordinarily prudent person in a like position would exercise under similar circumstances, and in a manner the Managing Member reasonably believes to be in the best interests of the Company.

**6.6 Indemnification.** The Company shall, to the fullest extent permitted by the Act, indemnify and hold harmless the Managing Member, officers, and employees of the Company from and against any loss, claim, damage, liability, or expense (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of any act or omission performed or omitted in good faith on behalf of the Company.

**7.1 Books and Records.** The Company shall maintain complete and accurate books and records of its business and affairs at its principal office, including those required by Corporations Code § 17701.13.

## ARTICLE 8 — TRANSFERS OF INTERESTS

**8.1 Restrictions on Transfer.** No Membership Interest may be transferred, assigned, pledged, or otherwise encumbered without the prior written consent of the Managing Member, except as expressly permitted by this Agreement.

**8.2 Transfers to Affiliates.** The Member may transfer all or a portion of his Membership Interest to a trust, family limited partnership, or other estate-planning vehicle for the benefit of himself or his immediate family, provided he retains voting and management control.

**8.3 Prohibited Transferees.** No transfer may be made to any party that would cause the Company to be in violation of the Pooling Agreement, the MSA, or applicable law.

---

## ARTICLE 9 — ADMISSION OF NEW MEMBERS

**9.1 Admission.** Additional members may be admitted to the Company only upon the written consent of the Managing Member and upon such terms and conditions as the Managing Member determines.

**9.2 Amendment on Admission.** Upon the admission of a new member, this Agreement shall be amended or restated to reflect the new member's Capital Contribution, Membership Interest, allocation rights, and any adjustments to governance.

**9.3 Coordination with Pooling Agreement.** The admission of any new member shall be a Reserved Matter under the Pooling Agreement, requiring prior written consent of Ariana Roberts as provided therein.

---

## ARTICLE 10 — DISSOLUTION AND WINDING UP

**10.1 Events of Dissolution.** The Company shall be dissolved upon the first to occur of:

(a) the written election of the Member to dissolve the Company;

(b) entry of a decree of judicial dissolution under the Act; or

(c) any other event causing dissolution under the Act.

**10.2 Winding Up.** Upon dissolution, the Managing Member (or a liquidator appointed by him) shall wind up the affairs of the Company, liquidate its assets, pay or provide for its liabilities, and distribute any remaining assets to the Member.

**10.3 Certificate of Cancellation.** Upon completion of winding up, the Company shall file a Certificate of Cancellation with the California Secretary of State.

**10.4 Coordination with Pooling Agreement.** Any dissolution shall be a Reserved Matter under the Pooling Agreement and, if it is part of a Liquidity Event, the distribution of proceeds shall comply with the pooling mechanics of the Pooling Agreement.

---

## ARTICLE 11 — AMENDMENTS

**11.1 Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by the Member. For so long as there is only one Member, the Member may amend this Agreement at any time, subject to the Reserved Matters provisions of the Pooling Agreement.
