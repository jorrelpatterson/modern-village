## PREAMBLE AND REGULATORY NOTE

The parties acknowledge that California's Moscone-Knox Professional Corporation Act (Corporations Code § 13400 et seq.) may not strictly require a Professional Corporation for a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA) practice, as California has historically lacked a dedicated professional-corporation authorization for behavior analysts. Accordingly, the parties have elected to conduct the clinical practice through a California limited liability company (an "LLC") rather than a Professional Corporation. Nevertheless, this Agreement imposes clinical-practice restrictions — including a requirement that ownership be held only by a duly licensed BCBA (or comparable licensed clinician permitted by California law) — in order to (a) preserve the clinical integrity of the practice, (b) prevent lay ownership or control of clinical decision-making, and (c) prepare the Company for potential future regulatory changes that may formalize the entity-form requirements for BCBA practices.

## ARTICLE 4 — MANAGEMENT OF THE CLINICAL LLC

**4.1 Manager-Managed LLC.** The Company is a manager-managed limited liability company. Ariana Roberts is hereby designated as the initial Sole Manager and shall serve until her resignation, death, disability, or removal by the Member.

**4.2 Officers.** The Sole Manager may designate officers of the Company, including titles customary for a clinical practice. The initial officers are:

- **President:** Ariana Roberts
- **Chief Clinical Officer:** Ariana Roberts
- **Secretary:** Ariana Roberts
- **Chief Financial Officer:** Ariana Roberts

The Sole Manager may appoint additional officers as needed, provided that officers who hold themselves out as rendering Professional Services must be Licensed Persons.

**4.3 Clinical Authority.** Consistent with Section 7.3 of the Term Sheet and California's prohibition on the corporate practice of medicine (as analogized to the practice of behavior analysis), the Sole Manager, in her clinical capacity, shall have exclusive and ultimate authority over:

(a) clinical service delivery and quality;

(b) clinician hiring, credentialing, supervision, discipline, and termination within the Company;

(c) development and approval of clinical training and continuing education content;

(d) HIPAA compliance, patient records management, and the content of clinical documentation;

(e) treatment planning and clinical decision-making for each patient or client;

(f) selection of diagnostic tools, assessment protocols, and clinical standards of care; and

(g) responses to clinical complaints, incidents, and licensing-board matters.

**4.4 Non-Clinical Support from MSO.** Pursuant to the MSA, the MSO provides the Company with non-clinical administrative, technology, marketing, billing, compliance support, and business-development services. The MSO shall not direct, supervise, or influence the practice of behavior analysis or the exercise of independent clinical judgment by any clinician of the Company.

**4.5 Action by Written Consent in Lieu of Meetings.** Because the Company has a Sole Member and a Sole Manager, no formal meetings are required. All actions that would otherwise require a member or manager vote may be taken by written consent signed by the Sole Member or Sole Manager, as applicable, and placed in the Company's records. The Company shall not be required to observe the corporate formalities applicable to a corporation (including annual shareholder meetings or board resolutions), although it shall maintain complete books and records as required by Section 5.1.

**4.6 Reserved Matters.** Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Sole Manager acknowledges that under the Pooling Agreement with Jorrel Patterson, certain "Reserved Matters" require the prior written consent of Jorrel Patterson as a contractual matter. The failure to obtain such consent shall be a breach of the Pooling Agreement and does not independently affect the Company's authority to act.

**4.7 Standard of Care.** The Manager shall discharge her duties in good faith, with the care an ordinarily prudent person in a like position would exercise under similar circumstances, and in a manner the Manager reasonably believes to be in the best interests of the Company.

**4.8 Indemnification.** The Company shall, to the fullest extent permitted by the Act, indemnify and hold harmless the Manager, officers, and employees of the Company from and against any loss, claim, damage, liability, or expense (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of any act or omission performed or omitted in good faith on behalf of the Company. Indemnification shall not extend to acts of gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breaches of clinical ethical obligations.

## ARTICLE 6 — RESTRICTIONS ON OWNERSHIP AND TRANSFER

**6.1 Ownership Limited to Licensed Persons.** All Membership Interest in the Company shall at all times be held only by Licensed Persons. While California law may not strictly require this restriction for a standard LLC rendering BCBA services, the Member has elected to impose this restriction contractually to preserve clinical integrity and to prepare for potential future regulatory changes.

**6.2 No Lay Ownership or Control.** No natural person or entity that is not a Licensed Person shall own, control, or beneficially hold any portion of the Membership Interest, the voting rights associated therewith, or the Company's Professional Services revenue beyond what is permitted under California law and the MSA.

**6.3 No Fee-Splitting.** No portion of the Company's fees for Professional Services shall be shared with any person or entity other than as permitted by law. The parties intend that all payments to the MSO under the MSA represent fair market value for bona fide administrative services and shall be structured accordingly.

**6.4 Disqualification.** If the Member ceases to be a Licensed Person (by reason of license lapse, suspension, revocation, death, or otherwise), the Membership Interest held by such Member shall be promptly transferred, redeemed, or otherwise disposed of to a qualified Licensed Person within a commercially reasonable period (and in any event not to exceed ninety (90) days, consistent with the standard the Moscone-Knox Act imposes on professional corporations, which the parties have voluntarily adopted here).

**6.5 Restrictions on Transfer.** No portion of the Membership Interest may be sold, transferred, pledged, hypothecated, gifted, or otherwise disposed of except:

(a) to another Licensed Person duly qualified under this Agreement; or

(b) to a trust or estate-planning vehicle that holds the Membership Interest solely for the benefit of a Licensed Person and that does not confer voting or management control on any non-Licensed Person.

Any purported transfer in violation of this Article 6 shall be null and void. Any membership certificate (or electronic record of ownership) shall bear a legend referencing this Agreement and the Buy-Sell Agreement.

**6.6 Right of First Refusal.** Any proposed transfer of any portion of the Membership Interest is subject to the right of first refusal in favor of the Company and Jorrel Patterson, as set forth in the Buy-Sell Agreement.
