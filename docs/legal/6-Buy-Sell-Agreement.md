# BUY-SELL AGREEMENT

**Among**
**Jorrel Patterson** ("Jorrel"),
**Ariana Roberts** ("Ariana"),
**Modern Village Services LLC** (the "MSO"), and
**Modern Village PC** (the "PC")

**Effective Date:** [DATE]

---

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, Jorrel owns one hundred percent (100%) of the membership interests of the MSO and Ariana owns one hundred percent (100%) of the shares of the PC;

WHEREAS, the MSO and the PC operate as a coordinated dual-entity structure pursuant to the Management Services Agreement and Pooling Agreement among the parties;

WHEREAS, the parties desire to establish orderly procedures for the purchase and sale of the MSO interests and PC shares upon the occurrence of certain triggering events — including death, disability, divorce, voluntary departure, and other events — in order to protect each party from unintended ownership changes and to preserve the continuity of the business; and

WHEREAS, the parties intend to fund the buy-out obligations in part through life insurance and structured payments.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein, the parties agree as follows:

---

## ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.**

- **"Affected Owner"** means the owner (Jorrel or Ariana) to whom a Triggering Event relates.
- **"Affected Interest"** means (i) in the case of Jorrel, his membership interest in the MSO, and (ii) in the case of Ariana, her shares of the PC.
- **"Closing"** has the meaning set forth in Section 6.
- **"Continuing Owner"** means the owner (Jorrel or Ariana) who is not the Affected Owner.
- **"Disability"** has the meaning set forth in Section 4.3.
- **"EBITDA"** means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization, determined in accordance with Section 5.
- **"Effective Date"** means the date first written above.
- **"Entity"** means the entity whose equity is subject to the Triggering Event (i.e., the MSO for Jorrel's interest or the PC for Ariana's shares).
- **"Purchase Price"** has the meaning set forth in Section 5.1.
- **"Triggering Event"** has the meaning set forth in Section 2.

---

## ARTICLE 2 — TRIGGERING EVENTS

**2.1 Triggering Events.** The following events shall trigger the buy-sell rights and obligations of this Agreement:

(a) **Death.** The death of either Jorrel or Ariana.

(b) **Permanent Disability.** The Disability of either Jorrel or Ariana, as determined under Section 4.3.

(c) **Divorce.** The entry of a final judgment or decree of divorce (or legal separation) that awards to a non-owner spouse any community property interest in the Affected Interest.

(d) **Voluntary Departure.** The voluntary resignation or withdrawal of either Jorrel (from active management of the MSO) or Ariana (from active service as Chief Clinical Officer of the PC).

(e) **Bankruptcy or Insolvency.** The filing of a voluntary or involuntary petition in bankruptcy by or against either Jorrel or Ariana, any general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or the attachment, levy, or judicial sale of the Affected Interest.

(f) **Attempted Unauthorized Transfer.** Any attempted transfer, pledge, or encumbrance of the Affected Interest that is not permitted by this Agreement, the MSO Operating Agreement, or the PC Shareholder Agreement.

**2.2 Notice of Triggering Event.** The Affected Owner (or her or his legal representative, estate, or spouse, as applicable) shall give written notice of the Triggering Event to the Continuing Owner and to the Entity within thirty (30) days after the occurrence of such event.

---

## ARTICLE 3 — BUY-OUT RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS

**3.1 Primary Structure: Cross-Purchase.** Upon a Triggering Event, the Continuing Owner shall have the right (and, in the case of death or Disability, the obligation) to purchase the Affected Interest from the Affected Owner (or his/her estate, spouse, or representative) at the Purchase Price determined under Section 5.

**3.2 Alternative: Entity Redemption.** If cross-purchase is not feasible due to cash constraints, legal restrictions, or other reasons reasonably determined by the Continuing Owner, the Entity itself may redeem the Affected Interest at the Purchase Price. In the case of the PC, any entity redemption must comply with California professional corporation rules.

**3.3 Rights vs. Obligations by Event Type.**

(a) **Death.** Cross-purchase is mandatory (to the extent funded by life insurance) and optional for any uninsured portion, subject to the Continuing Owner's election within 180 days.

(b) **Permanent Disability.** Cross-purchase or redemption is at the option of the Continuing Owner or the Entity, exercisable within 180 days after a final determination of Disability.

(c) **Divorce.** The Continuing Owner has a right of first refusal to purchase any community property interest awarded to a non-owner spouse, exercisable within 90 days after notice of the divorce judgment.

(d) **Voluntary Departure.** The Continuing Owner has an option (but not an obligation) to purchase the Affected Interest at the Purchase Price, exercisable within 180 days after the departure date.

(e) **Bankruptcy or Insolvency.** The Continuing Owner has an option to purchase the Affected Interest at the Purchase Price, exercisable within 90 days after notice of the bankruptcy or insolvency event.

(f) **Attempted Unauthorized Transfer.** The purported transferee shall acquire no rights; the Continuing Owner has the right to purchase the Affected Interest at the Purchase Price.

**3.4 Compliance with Professional Corporation Rules.** Any purchase of PC shares must be made only by a Licensed Person permitted to own shares in a California professional corporation practicing behavior analysis. If Jorrel (as Continuing Owner) is not a Licensed Person, the PC shall redeem the shares or Jorrel shall designate a qualified purchaser acceptable to Ariana or her estate.

---

## ARTICLE 4 — DISABILITY

**4.1 Definition of Disability.** "Disability" means a physical or mental condition that renders the Affected Owner unable to perform the material duties of his or her position with the Entity for a continuous period of one hundred eighty (180) days, as determined in accordance with Section 4.2.

**4.2 Determination.** A Disability determination shall be made as follows:

(a) The Continuing Owner may request a Disability determination by written notice.

(b) The Affected Owner shall submit to an independent medical examination ("IME") by a licensed physician selected by mutual agreement, or if the parties cannot agree, by a physician designated by the President of the Los Angeles County Medical Association.

(c) The IME report shall be binding on the parties absent manifest error.

(d) The Affected Owner shall cooperate with the IME process; unreasonable refusal to cooperate shall support a finding of Disability.

**4.3 Cost of IME.** The cost of the IME shall be borne by the Entity.

---

## ARTICLE 5 — VALUATION OF AFFECTED INTEREST

**5.1 Purchase Price Formula.** Consistent with Section 8.7 of the Term Sheet, the Purchase Price for the Affected Interest is equal to:

**(a) Book Value of the Entity, plus (b) three (3) times the trailing twelve (12) months' EBITDA of the Entity**,

multiplied by the Affected Owner's percentage ownership in the Entity as of the Triggering Event date.

**5.2 Book Value.** "Book Value" means the Entity's total assets minus total liabilities, as shown on the Entity's most recent financial statements prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles ("GAAP") (or a consistently applied cash-basis method if GAAP is not used), as of the last day of the month immediately preceding the Triggering Event.

**5.3 EBITDA.** "EBITDA" means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization for the trailing twelve calendar months ending on the last day of the month immediately preceding the Triggering Event, computed consistently with the Entity's accounting practices and adjusted for:

(a) one-time or non-recurring items;

(b) related-party transactions other than the MSA Management Fee (which shall be treated as an ordinary operating expense); and

(c) owner compensation in excess of fair market value for services rendered.

**5.4 Appraisal Upon Disagreement.** If the parties disagree on the calculation of Book Value or EBITDA:

(a) Each party shall appoint a qualified appraiser (CPA or business valuation specialist) within thirty (30) days.

(b) The two appraisers shall attempt to reach agreement.

(c) If they cannot, they shall jointly appoint a third appraiser, whose determination shall be the midpoint of the two values that are closest together, or if all three values are equidistant, the middle value.

(d) The cost of the appraisal process shall be shared equally.

**5.5 Goodwill.** The 3x EBITDA multiplier is intended to capture enterprise goodwill. No separate goodwill adjustment shall be made.

## ARTICLE 6 — PAYMENT TERMS AND CLOSING

**6.1 Closing.** The Closing shall occur within sixty (60) days after the Purchase Price is determined (including resolution of any appraisal process), at a time and place mutually agreed.

**6.2 Lump Sum from Insurance.** To the extent the Purchase Price is funded by life insurance proceeds, the insured portion shall be paid in cash at Closing.

**6.3 Promissory Note for Uninsured Portion.** Any portion of the Purchase Price not funded by insurance shall be paid pursuant to a promissory note executed at Closing with the following terms:

(a) **Term:** five (5) years;

(b) **Amortization:** equal monthly installments of principal and interest;

(c) **Interest Rate:** the applicable federal rate ("AFR") for mid-term instruments in effect on the Closing date, plus one percent (1%);

(d) **Prepayment:** permitted at any time without penalty;

(e) **Default:** upon default and failure to cure within thirty (30) days, the unpaid balance shall accelerate and become immediately due; and

(f) **Security:** the promissory note shall be secured by the Affected Interest (held in escrow or subject to a lien) until fully paid.

**6.4 Transfer Documents.** At Closing, the Affected Owner (or representative) shall deliver duly executed transfer documents (assignment, stock power, or equivalent), along with all certificates, keys, and records related to the Affected Interest.

**6.5 Releases.** At Closing, the parties shall exchange mutual releases of claims arising under this Agreement with respect to the Affected Interest, other than post-closing obligations under the promissory note.

---

## ARTICLE 7 — LIFE INSURANCE FUNDING

**7.1 Required Policies.** Each of Jorrel and Ariana shall apply for and maintain a cross-purchase life insurance policy on the life of the other, as follows:

(a) **Initial Face Amount:** not less than one million dollars ($1,000,000) each.

(b) **Ownership:** each party owns and is the beneficiary of the policy on the other party's life.

(c) **Premium Payment:** each party pays the premiums on the policy he or she owns. Premiums may be reimbursed by the applicable Entity if the Entity's board or managing member so approves.

(d) **Policy Type:** term or permanent, at the discretion of each policy owner.

**7.2 Annual Review.** The parties shall review the adequacy of insurance coverage annually in connection with the Entity's financial-statement close, and shall adjust face amounts as reasonably necessary to approximate the projected Purchase Price.

**7.3 Disability Insurance.** The parties may, but are not required to, obtain disability insurance to fund Disability buy-outs. Any such insurance shall be held on the same cross-purchase or entity basis as life insurance, at the parties' election.

**7.4 Failure to Obtain Insurance.** If a party is uninsurable or unable to obtain insurance at standard rates, the parties shall cooperate in good faith to establish an alternative funding mechanism (e.g., a sinking fund) or to adjust the Purchase Price formula accordingly.

**7.5 Insurance Proceeds.** Insurance proceeds received upon the death of an owner shall be applied first to the Purchase Price under this Agreement. Any excess proceeds shall belong to the policy owner.

---

## ARTICLE 8 — DIVORCE PROVISIONS

**8.1 Spousal Waiver at Signing.** Each party shall cause his or her spouse (if any) at the time of signing, and any subsequent spouse, to execute a Spousal Consent acknowledging that any community property interest in the Affected Interest shall be subject to this Agreement.

**8.2 Right of First Refusal.** If a divorce court awards any portion of the Affected Interest to a non-owner spouse, the Continuing Owner shall have a right of first refusal to purchase such portion at the price determined under Section 5, exercisable within ninety (90) days after notice.

**8.3 No Voting Rights for Non-Owner Spouse.** Pending exercise of the ROFR, the non-owner spouse shall have no voting rights, no management rights, and no rights to information beyond the financial economic interest.

---

## ARTICLE 9 — TRANSFER RESTRICTIONS

**9.1 No Transfers Outside This Agreement.** No Affected Interest may be transferred except in accordance with this Agreement, the MSO Operating Agreement, and the PC Shareholder Agreement, and any attempted transfer in violation of these agreements shall be null and void.

**9.2 Right of First Refusal on Voluntary Sales.** If either Jorrel or Ariana receives a bona fide offer to purchase all or part of the Affected Interest during lifetime (and such sale is not otherwise prohibited by the Pooling Agreement or the PC Shareholder Agreement), the Continuing Owner shall have a right of first refusal for thirty (30) days to match the third-party offer, consistent with Section 8.4 of the Term Sheet.

**9.3 Drag-Along and Tag-Along.** The drag-along and tag-along rights set forth in Sections 8.5 and 8.6 of the Term Sheet are hereby incorporated by reference and remain binding on the parties and their successors.
