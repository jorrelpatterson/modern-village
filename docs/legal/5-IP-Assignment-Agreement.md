# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

**Between**
**Jorrel Patterson** ("Assignor")
**and**

**Effective Date:** [DATE]

---

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, Assignor has, since prior to the formation of Assignee, conceived, developed, authored, and owned all right, title, and interest in and to a body of intellectual property, technology, brand, and business assets comprising the Modern Village neurodivergent parenting platform and its related operations;

WHEREAS, Assignee is a newly formed California limited liability company whose sole member is Assignor and which is the operating entity for the non-clinical business of Modern Village;

WHEREAS, it is the intent of Assignor and Assignee that, effective as of the Effective Date, Assignor irrevocably assign and transfer to Assignee all of Assignor's right, title, and interest in and to the Transferred IP (defined below), so that Assignee may own and operate such assets free of any claim by Assignor; and

WHEREAS, such assignment constitutes Assignor's initial capital contribution to Assignee under the Assignee's Operating Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

---

## ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.**

- **"Assignor"** means Jorrel Patterson, individually.
- **"Assignee"** means Modern Village Services LLC, a California limited liability company.
- **"Effective Date"** means the date on which this Agreement is effective upon execution by all parties whose signature is required below.
- **"Transferred IP"** has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1.

---

## ARTICLE 2 — ASSIGNMENT

**2.1 Transferred IP.** Assignor hereby irrevocably assigns, transfers, conveys, and delivers to Assignee all of Assignor's right, title, and interest, worldwide and in perpetuity, in and to the following (collectively, the "Transferred IP"):

(a) **Software and Source Code.** All software programs, source code, object code, scripts, databases, schemas, migrations, configuration files, deployment artifacts, and documentation comprising the Modern Village platform, including without limitation:

(i) the consumer application, marketing site, and blog front-end (including but not limited to `index.html`, `app.html`, `blog.html`, `district-one-pager.html`, `screener.html`, `privacy.html`, `terms.html`, `baa.html`, `favicon.svg`);

(ii) the administrative console and dashboards (including but not limited to `admin.html`, `district-admin.html`);

(iii) all server-side code, workers, functions, and APIs (including but not limited to the Cloudflare Worker and any supporting scripts, e.g., `worker.js`);

(iv) all database schemas, SQL migrations, seed files, and Supabase configuration;

(v) all scraper tools and their outputs (including but not limited to `scraper.js`, `scraper-districts.js`, `scraper-grants.js`, `scraper-extra.js`, `combine.js`, the scraper toolkit, and associated data files);

(vi) all components, libraries, utilities, and build configurations;

(vii) all automated test code, fixtures, and QA tooling; and

(viii) all documentation, specs, READMEs, playbooks, and design documents relating to the foregoing.

(b) **Brand and Trademarks.** The "Modern Village" name, word mark, logo, color palette, design system, trade dress, and any associated trademark applications or registrations (whether filed or unfiled), and all goodwill associated therewith.

(c) **Domain Names.** All domain names owned or controlled by Assignor in connection with Modern Village, including without limitation `modernvillage.app` and any subdomains, redirects, or associated DNS configurations.

(d) **Marketing Materials.** All marketing copy, graphics, photography, video, audio, email templates, landing pages, ad creatives, social media content, scripts, sales decks, pitch materials, and educational materials created in connection with Modern Village.

(e) **Customer and Lead Data.** All customer data, CRM records, contact lists, lead lists, email lists, subscriber records, survey responses, onboarding records, and user-generated content collected through the Modern Village platform (subject to applicable privacy law and user consent).

(f) **Scraper Outputs and Data Sets.** All data sets produced by the scrapers, including the California school district lead file (`modern-village-ca-district-leads.xlsx`), regional center data, grant data, and associated enrichment.

(g) **Business Plans and Strategy.** All business plans, roadmaps, strategy documents, marketing strategies, sales playbooks, competitive analyses, financial projections, and related written materials (including the documents in the project's `_reference` directory).

(h) **Trade Secrets and Know-How.** All trade secrets, know-how, methods, processes, techniques, and confidential information developed by Assignor in connection with Modern Village.

(i) **Goodwill.** All goodwill associated with the foregoing.

(j) **Derivative and Ancillary Rights.** All past, present, and future causes of action and remedies for infringement, misappropriation, or other violation of any of the foregoing, and all rights to sue and recover for such violations.

**2.2 Nature of Assignment.** The assignment in Section 2.1 is:

(a) **Irrevocable.** Once effective, the assignment cannot be rescinded by Assignor.

(b) **Perpetual.** The assignment lasts for the full duration of any applicable intellectual property rights.

(c) **Worldwide.** The assignment covers all jurisdictions globally.

(d) **Exclusive.** Assignor retains no rights, licenses, or interests in the Transferred IP, express or implied.

**2.3 Further Assurances.** Assignor shall, upon Assignee's request and without additional consideration, execute and deliver such further documents, instruments, and assurances (including patent, trademark, and copyright assignment forms) as may be reasonably necessary or appropriate to perfect, record, or enforce Assignee's ownership of the Transferred IP, including before the United States Patent and Trademark Office, the United States Copyright Office, domain registrars, and any foreign intellectual property authority.

**2.4 Power of Attorney.** Assignor hereby appoints Assignee and its duly authorized officers as Assignor's attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to execute any document necessary to perfect the assignment described herein if Assignor fails or refuses to do so within a reasonable time after request. This power of attorney is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

**2.5 Moral Rights Waiver.** To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, Assignor irrevocably waives, and agrees not to assert against Assignee or its successors, licensees, and assigns, any "moral rights," "droit moral," or similar rights in the Transferred IP.

**2.6 No Implied Licenses.** Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to grant Assignor any license, express or implied, to use the Transferred IP, except as Assignor may be authorized to do so in his capacity as Managing Member or employee of Assignee.
