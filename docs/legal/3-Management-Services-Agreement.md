# MANAGEMENT SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Between**
**Modern Village Services LLC** ("MSO")
**and**
**Modern Village LLC** (the "Clinical LLC")

**Effective Date:** [DATE]

---

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, the MSO is a California limited liability company that owns and operates a technology platform, brand assets, marketing operations, billing systems, and related non-clinical business infrastructure supporting the delivery of behavior-analytic services to neurodivergent families;

WHEREAS, the Clinical LLC is a California limited liability company (already formed and in good standing) through which Ariana Roberts, a Board Certified Behavior Analyst, and other duly licensed clinicians render behavior-analytic professional services, including Parent Consultation services billed under CPT code 97156;

WHEREAS, the Clinical LLC desires to focus its resources on clinical care and therefore desires to engage the MSO to provide comprehensive, arm's-length non-clinical management services;

WHEREAS, the parties intend that this Agreement comply with California law (including the California Revised Uniform Limited Liability Company Act and the principles underlying the corporate practice of medicine doctrine as analogized to behavior-analytic services), applicable federal law, and applicable payor contracts, and that all fees paid under this Agreement represent fair market value for bona fide administrative services; and

WHEREAS, the parties are separate legal entities, each with its own independent governance, and neither is an agent, partner, or joint venturer of the other.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein, the parties agree as follows:

---

## ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.**

- **"Affiliate"** means, as to any person, any other person directly or indirectly controlling, controlled by, or under common control with such person.
- **"Agreement"** means this Management Services Agreement.
- **"BAA"** means a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement entered into between the parties concurrently or promptly after the Effective Date.
- **"Clinical Matter"** means any matter involving (i) the exercise of professional judgment in the care of a patient or client, (ii) treatment planning, (iii) clinical documentation, (iv) diagnosis or assessment, (v) licensure or professional discipline, or (vi) any other matter that must, by law or ethics, be decided by a Licensed Person.
- **"Effective Date"** means the date on which this Agreement is effective upon execution by all parties whose signature is required below.
- **"Gross Revenue of the Clinical LLC"** means all amounts actually received by the Clinical LLC from the delivery of Professional Services, net of (a) refunds to patients, (b) bad debt write-offs, (c) payor chargebacks, and (d) amounts collected but owed to third parties. Gross Revenue of the Clinical LLC excludes (i) loans to the Clinical LLC, (ii) capital contributions, and (iii) insurance proceeds not related to services rendered.
- **"Licensed Person"** has the meaning set forth in the Clinical LLC Operating Agreement.
- **"Management Fee"** has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.
- **"Management Services"** has the meaning set forth in Article 2.
- **"Pooling Agreement"** means the Pooling Agreement between Jorrel Patterson and Ariana Roberts dated as of the Effective Date.
- **"PHI"** means Protected Health Information as defined under HIPAA.
- **"Professional Services"** means the behavior-analytic services rendered by the Clinical LLC through its Licensed Persons.

## ARTICLE 2 — MANAGEMENT SERVICES

**2.1 Services Provided by the MSO.** Subject to and consistent with Section 2.1 of the Term Sheet, the MSO shall provide the following non-clinical Management Services to the Clinical LLC during the Term:

(a) **Technology Platform.** Hosting, maintenance, development, and support of the Modern Village software platform, including the consumer application, administrative console, district portal, provider marketplace, automation tooling, and related APIs and integrations.

(b) **Marketing and Advertising.** Development and execution of marketing campaigns; creation of marketing collateral; management of social-media and content channels; advertising spend management; SEO and organic acquisition.

(c) **Lead Generation and Business Development.** Operation of lead-generation pipelines, scrapers, CRM systems; coordination of outreach to districts, regional centers, corporate partners, and payors; maintenance of lead lists and conversion workflows.

(d) **Billing and Collections.** Non-clinical billing and collections support including claims-submission workflow, payment reconciliation, denial management tooling, and patient invoicing systems. (For the avoidance of doubt, final decisions on coding and clinical content of claims remain with the Clinical LLC and its clinicians.)

(e) **Customer Support.** Operation of non-clinical customer support channels, including in-app help, ticketing, chat support, and escalation coordination.

(f) **Administrative Services.** Human resources support for the Clinical LLC's non-clinical needs; bookkeeping, accounting, and financial-reporting support; regulatory-compliance support (non-clinical); vendor management; insurance procurement support; records management (non-clinical).

(g) **Training and Educational Tools.** Provision of training platforms, continuing-education infrastructure, and course-delivery tools that the Clinical LLC's clinicians may, in their discretion, use to deliver clinical training. (The clinical content and accreditation of any continuing education remain the Clinical LLC's responsibility.)

(h) **Provider Marketplace Infrastructure.** Operation of the marketplace platform connecting third-party providers with families.

(i) **Other Services.** Such other non-clinical services as the parties may mutually agree in writing from time to time.

**2.2 Services Expressly Not Provided.** Notwithstanding Section 2.1, the MSO shall not, and shall have no authority to:

(a) direct, supervise, or control Clinical Matters or the exercise of independent professional judgment by any Licensed Person;

(b) hire, fire, discipline, credential, supervise, or set compensation for any clinician of the Clinical LLC (non-clinical support staff of the Clinical LLC shall remain under the Clinical LLC's control although the MSO may provide HR tools and recommendations);

(c) control, alter, or dictate the content of any clinical documentation, treatment plan, assessment, or billing code;

(d) determine which patients or clients the Clinical LLC accepts or discharges, or the appropriate course of care for any patient;

(e) possess, practice, or offer to the public any professional licensure owned by the Clinical LLC; or

(f) engage in the practice of behavior analysis.

**2.3 Clinical LLC Retains Clinical Authority.** The Clinical LLC retains full, exclusive, and independent authority over all Clinical Matters as more fully set forth in the Clinical LLC Operating Agreement.

**2.4 Standard of Performance.** The MSO shall provide the Management Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, consistent with reasonable industry standards for management service organizations supporting behavioral health practices.

## ARTICLE 3 — FACILITIES, EQUIPMENT, AND PERSONNEL

**3.1 Facilities.** The MSO may, at the Clinical LLC's request, assist the Clinical LLC in identifying, leasing, or subleasing office space. Any lease to the Clinical LLC shall be at fair market value.

**3.2 Equipment and Supplies.** The MSO may procure non-clinical equipment, software licenses, and supplies necessary for the Clinical LLC's operations at cost plus a reasonable administrative markup representing fair market value.

**3.3 Non-Clinical Personnel.** The MSO employs its own workforce. If the Clinical LLC requires dedicated non-clinical personnel (e.g., front-office staff), such personnel may be provided by the MSO as contractors or loaned employees, provided their work is non-clinical.

**3.4 Clinical Personnel.** All clinicians of the Clinical LLC shall be employed or engaged by the Clinical LLC. The MSO shall not employ, contract with, or otherwise engage clinicians to provide Professional Services on behalf of the Clinical LLC.

---

## ARTICLE 4 — MANAGEMENT FEE

**4.1 Management Fee.** Consistent with Section 2.2 of the Term Sheet, the Clinical LLC shall pay the MSO a Management Fee equal to fifty percent (50%) of the Gross Revenue of the Clinical LLC for each calendar month during the Term, with a minimum floor of five hundred dollars ($500) per month regardless of actual Gross Revenue of the Clinical LLC.

**4.2 Billing and Payment.**

(a) The MSO shall deliver an invoice to the Clinical LLC within ten (10) business days after the end of each calendar month, setting forth the calculation of the Management Fee for such month.

(b) The Clinical LLC shall pay the Management Fee within fifteen (15) business days after receipt of the invoice. Payment shall be made by wire transfer or ACH to an account designated by the MSO.

(c) Late amounts shall accrue interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law.

**4.3 Annual Review and Adjustment.** The parties shall review the Management Fee annually. The Management Fee may be adjusted only upon the mutual written agreement of the parties, and any adjustment shall continue to represent fair market value for the services actually provided.

**4.4 Fair Market Value Representation.** The parties represent and warrant that:

(a) the Management Fee was negotiated at arm's length;

(b) the Management Fee represents, in the reasonable business judgment of both parties, fair market value for the Management Services provided;

(c) the Management Fee is not intended to induce or reward referrals of patients or the generation of business; and

(d) neither party has agreed to pay or receive remuneration in exchange for referrals in violation of the federal Anti-Kickback Statute, the federal Stark Law (to the extent applicable), the California anti-referral law (Business and Professions Code § 650 et seq.), or any other applicable law.

**4.5 Periodic FMV Validation.** The parties shall, at least once every three years, obtain or commission a fair market value analysis supporting the continuation of the Management Fee, and shall retain such analysis in their records.

## ARTICLE 5 — REVENUE ALLOCATION

**5.1 Clinical LLC Revenue.** The Clinical LLC shall bill and collect from patients, clients, and third-party payors for all Professional Services. All such revenue shall be deposited initially into an account controlled by the Clinical LLC.

**5.2 MSO Revenue.** The MSO retains one hundred percent (100%) of (i) parent subscription revenue, (ii) marketplace fees from third-party providers, (iii) district contracts, (iv) grant funding, and (v) any other non-clinical revenue, all consistent with Section 2.3 of the Term Sheet.

**5.3 No Commingling.** Neither party shall commingle its revenue with the revenue of the other.

---

## ARTICLE 6 — HIPAA AND CONFIDENTIALITY

**6.1 HIPAA Business Associate Agreement.** To the extent the MSO creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on behalf of the Clinical LLC, the parties shall enter into a separate HIPAA Business Associate Agreement that meets the requirements of 45 C.F.R. § 164.504(e). The BAA is incorporated herein by reference.

**6.2 Confidentiality.** Each party shall treat as confidential all non-public business and clinical information of the other party, and shall not disclose or use such information except as permitted by this Agreement, the BAA, or applicable law. This obligation survives termination for five (5) years (indefinitely as to trade secrets and PHI).

**6.3 Data Ownership.**

(a) PHI and clinical records are, and at all times remain, the property of the Clinical LLC, subject only to the MSO's rights as a business associate.

(b) Non-clinical business data (e.g., marketing leads, CRM contacts, subscription records) created by or for the MSO remain the property of the MSO.

## ARTICLE 7 — TERM AND TERMINATION

**7.1 Initial Term.** The initial term of this Agreement is five (5) years beginning on the Effective Date (the "Initial Term").

**7.2 Renewal.** Following the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms (each, a "Renewal Term," together with the Initial Term, the "Term"), unless either party delivers written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred twenty (120) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

**7.3 Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement for cause if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice specifying the breach. In the case of a breach that cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, the breaching party shall have a reasonable additional cure period so long as it is diligently pursuing cure.

**7.4 Termination for Convenience.** Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon ninety (90) days' prior written notice to the other party; provided that, because termination of this Agreement would be a Reserved Matter likely to impair the Pooling Agreement, any termination for convenience shall be subject to the consent requirements of the Pooling Agreement.

**7.5 Automatic Termination.** This Agreement shall terminate automatically upon:

(a) the dissolution, liquidation, or wind-up of either party;

(b) the loss by the Clinical LLC of its ability to lawfully render Professional Services in California; or

(c) a final, non-appealable determination by a governmental authority that this Agreement violates applicable law in a manner not curable by amendment.

**7.6 Effect of Termination.**

(a) Upon termination, the MSO shall cooperate with the Clinical LLC to ensure an orderly transition of Management Services and non-disruption of patient care for a period of up to one hundred eighty (180) days (the "Transition Period"), and the Clinical LLC shall continue to pay the Management Fee (or a prorated portion agreed by the parties) during the Transition Period.

(b) Clinical records and PHI shall remain with the Clinical LLC. Non-clinical business data shall remain with the MSO.

(c) Each party shall return or destroy the other party's confidential information in accordance with Section 6 and the BAA.

(d) Termination shall not affect obligations that by their nature survive termination (e.g., indemnification, confidentiality, dispute resolution).

## ARTICLE 8 — INDEMNIFICATION

**8.1 MSO Indemnification.** The MSO shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Clinical LLC and its members, managers, officers, employees, and agents from and against any and all claims, losses, damages, liabilities, and reasonable attorneys' fees arising out of (a) the MSO's negligent acts or omissions, (b) the MSO's breach of this Agreement, or (c) any claim that the MSO's services infringe third-party intellectual property rights.

**8.2 Clinical LLC Indemnification.** The Clinical LLC shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the MSO and its members, officers, employees, and agents from and against any and all claims, losses, damages, liabilities, and reasonable attorneys' fees arising out of (a) the delivery of Professional Services (including any claim of professional malpractice or patient injury), (b) the Clinical LLC's breach of this Agreement, or (c) the Clinical LLC's negligent acts or omissions.

**8.3 Procedures.** The indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any indemnifiable claim and shall cooperate in the defense. The indemnifying party shall have the right to control the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party.

## ARTICLE 9 — INSURANCE

**9.1 MSO Insurance.** The MSO shall maintain, at its own expense, (a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 aggregate, (b) cyber liability and technology errors and omissions insurance of at least $2,000,000, and (c) employer's liability and workers' compensation as required by law.

**9.2 Clinical LLC Insurance.** The Clinical LLC shall maintain, at its own expense, (a) professional liability (malpractice) insurance covering each of its clinicians in amounts standard for California BCBA practices and not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence / $3,000,000 aggregate, (b) commercial general liability insurance, and (c) employer's liability and workers' compensation as required by law.

**9.3 Certificates.** Each party shall furnish the other with certificates of insurance on request.

## ARTICLE 10 — COMPLIANCE WITH LAWS

**10.1 General.** Each party shall comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws, rules, regulations, and orders, including without limitation HIPAA, the federal Anti-Kickback Statute (42 U.S.C. § 1320a-7b), the California Anti-Kickback/Fee-Splitting statute (B&P Code § 650), the California Revised Uniform Limited Liability Company Act, and California payor laws.

**10.2 Corporate Practice Compliance.** The parties shall structure their activities under this Agreement in a manner consistent with California's prohibitions on the corporate practice of medicine (as analogized to the practice of behavior analysis) and on unlawful fee-splitting.

**10.3 Modification for Compliance.** If a change in applicable law reasonably requires amendment to preserve compliance, the parties shall negotiate in good faith to amend this Agreement to preserve economic intent while achieving compliance.

## ARTICLE 11 — INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR; NO AGENCY

**11.1 Independent Contractor.** The MSO is an independent contractor to the Clinical LLC. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create a partnership, joint venture, employer-employee relationship, or agency relationship between the parties. Neither party has authority to bind the other.

**11.2 No Referral Obligation.** Nothing in this Agreement requires either party to refer patients or business to the other.

---

## ARTICLE 11A — STRIPE ACCOUNT TRANSITION

**11A.1 Transitional Stripe Account.** The parties acknowledge that at the time of this Agreement's execution, parent subscription revenue is processed through a Stripe payment account owned by a separate entity controlled by Jorrel Patterson ("Transitional Stripe Account"). Within sixty (60) days of the Effective Date, Jorrel shall transfer or establish a new Stripe account owned solely by the MSO, at which point all Modern Village subscription revenue shall route directly to the MSO. During the transition period, the Transitional Stripe Account shall operate as a pass-through: all Modern Village subscription revenue shall be remitted to the MSO within seven (7) days of receipt, with no markup, fee, or retention. The parties shall maintain contemporaneous records of all transitional revenue flows and make such records available for audit by Ariana upon request.

**12.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by California law, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

**12.2 Dispute Resolution.** Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be resolved in accordance with Section 11 of the Term Sheet (informal resolution, mediation, and binding arbitration in Los Angeles County, California under JAMS rules).
