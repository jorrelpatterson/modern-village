## 1. Structure Overview

Modern Village shall operate as a **dual-entity MSO / Clinical LLC structure**:

### 1.1 Modern Village Services LLC (the "MSO")
- **Entity type:** California LLC
- **Owner:** Jorrel Patterson, 100%
- **Purpose:** Owns and operates all technology, platform, brand, intellectual property, marketing, business development, and non-clinical operations of Modern Village
- **Assets owned by MSO:**
  - All software, code, databases, and technical infrastructure (app.html, admin.html, worker.js, all supporting files)
  - The "Modern Village" brand, trademarks, domain names, and marketing materials
  - All CRM data, lead lists, marketing contacts, and scraper outputs
  - Stripe payment processing account and subscription revenue
  - Customer support operations and non-clinical employees
- **Liability exposure:** Business, technology, marketing, and operational liability only. Zero clinical liability.

### 1.2 Modern Village LLC (the Clinical LLC)
- **Entity type:** California Limited Liability Company
- **Status:** **Already formed as of [DATE] — Ariana filed the LLC**
- **Sole Member:** Ariana Roberts, 100%
- **Purpose:** Holds all clinical licenses, employs clinicians, provides clinical services, and bills insurance
- **Assets owned by the Clinical LLC:**
  - Ariana's BCBA license and any clinical credentials
  - Patient/client records and clinical documentation
  - Clinical staff employment contracts
  - Insurance billing relationships (CPT 97156 Parent Consultation contracts)
- **Liability exposure:** All clinical malpractice, HIPAA, and patient-related liability

> **Note:** California does not currently require a Professional Corporation structure for BCBA practices. The LLC form is used with contractual ownership restrictions (members must be licensed BCBAs) to preserve clinical practice integrity.

## 2. Management Services Agreement (MSA)

### 2.1 Services Provided by MSO to the Clinical LLC
The MSO shall provide the Clinical LLC with a comprehensive platform including:
- Software and technology platform
- Marketing and lead generation
- Billing systems and administrative support
- Customer support and client-facing operations
- Provider marketplace infrastructure
- Training and continuing education tools

### 2.2 Management Fee
- **Structure:** 50% of Clinical LLC gross revenue, paid monthly
- **Floor:** Minimum monthly fee of $500 regardless of Clinical LLC revenue (prevents the Clinical LLC from freeloading)
- **Adjustment:** Fee may be renegotiated annually upon mutual written agreement

### 2.3 Revenue Allocation (Day-to-Day)
- **MSO retains 100% of:** Parent subscriptions, marketplace fees from third-party providers, district contracts, grant funding, any non-clinical revenue
- **Clinical LLC retains 50% of:** Clinical session revenue after the MSA fee is paid to MSO (the other 50% flows to MSO as management fee)
- **Each party draws salary/distributions from their own entity**

## 3. Pooling Agreement (Sale Proceeds)

### 3.1 Combined Proceeds Pooling
Upon any Liquidity Event (defined below), the combined net proceeds from the sale of both entities shall be pooled and distributed according to this Section.

### 3.2 Definition of Liquidity Event
A "Liquidity Event" includes:
- Sale of substantially all assets of either or both entities
- Merger or consolidation with another company
- Change of control of either or both entities
- Dissolution and distribution of assets

### 3.3 Capital Recovery (Paid Before Pooling)
Before pooling the combined proceeds, Jorrel shall be reimbursed for:
- Cash marketing spend (ads, content, CRM tools, scrapers, conferences)
- Technology infrastructure costs (hosting, APIs, software licenses)
- Outside contractors and service providers
- Legal and accounting fees
- Personal capital contributions to the MSO

**Capital Recovery Cap:** The lesser of (a) actual documented spend, or (b) 20% of total sale proceeds, or (c) $500,000.

**Documentation requirement:** All reimbursable expenses must be supported by invoices, receipts, or bank records. A running capital recovery ledger shall be maintained and shared with Ariana quarterly.

### 3.4 50/50 Split of Remaining Proceeds
After Capital Recovery and investor distributions, the remaining proceeds shall be split 50/50 between Jorrel and Ariana, subject to the Vesting Schedule in Section 4.

### 3.5 Worked Example
- Combined sale: $10,000,000 ($9M MSO + $1M Clinical LLC)
- Investor holdings: 20% of MSO = $1,800,000 off the top
- Subtotal: $8,200,000
- Capital Recovery to Jorrel: $300,000
- Subtotal: $7,900,000
- 50/50 split: $3,950,000 each
- **Jorrel total:** $3,950,000 + $300,000 = $4,250,000
- **Ariana total:** $3,950,000

## 4. Vesting Schedule

### 4.1 Ariana's Pooling Interest
Ariana's share of the 50/50 pooling under Section 3.4 vests monthly over 48 months:
- Vesting begins on the Effective Date of this agreement
- **1/48 per month** (approximately 2.08%) of continuous active service
- No cliff — vesting begins immediately in month 1
- Full vesting achieved at month 48

### 4.2 Acceleration on Sale (If Still Active)
**If Ariana is Actively Contributing at the time of a Liquidity Event, her pooling interest shall be deemed 100% vested at the moment of the sale, regardless of elapsed time.**

### 4.3 Definition of "Actively Contributing"
Ariana is "Actively Contributing" if ALL of the following are true:
- (a) She is employed by Modern Village LLC (the Clinical LLC) as Chief Clinical Officer
- (b) She is providing a minimum of 10 hours per week of services to the Clinical LLC
- (c) She has not given notice of resignation
- (d) She has not been terminated for Cause (defined below)

### 4.4 Vesting Upon Departure
If Ariana ceases Actively Contributing before a Liquidity Event, her vesting locks at her then-current percentage. She keeps her vested portion; any unvested portion reverts to Jorrel's side of the pooling agreement.

### 4.5 Worked Examples

**Scenario A — Still active at sale (month 24):**
- Vesting on paper: 50%
- Actual vesting (with acceleration): **100%** (still active)
- Ariana receives full 50% pooling share

**Scenario B — Quit at month 10 (good leaver), sale at month 24:**
- Vesting locked at month 10: ~20.8%
- Ariana receives 20.8% of her 50% = **~10.4% of pooled proceeds**
- Jorrel receives remaining ~89.6%

**Scenario C — Fired for Cause at month 10, sale at month 24:**
- Cause termination → forfeits ALL vested shares
- Ariana receives **$0** from pooling
- She keeps only her Clinical LLC and its standalone value
- Jorrel receives 100% of MSO proceeds + any Clinical LLC proceeds from buy-sell

## 5. Good Leaver / Bad Leaver Provisions

### 5.1 Good Leaver Events (Vested amount retained)
- Termination by Jorrel without Cause
- Death
- Permanent disability (unable to perform duties for 6+ months)
- Mutual separation agreed in writing

### 5.2 Bad Leaver Events (All shares forfeited, including vested)
- **Termination for Cause:** Theft, fraud, willful misconduct, conviction of a crime of moral turpitude, gross negligence in clinical duties, material breach of this agreement
- Breach of non-compete or non-solicit provisions (Section 8)
- Voluntary resignation to work at a direct competitor within 2 years

### 5.3 Voluntary Resignation (Non-Competitor)
If Ariana voluntarily resigns to pursue an unrelated opportunity (not a direct competitor):
- She is treated as a Good Leaver
- Her vested shares are retained
- Vesting stops immediately

## 6. Origination Commissions

Ariana shall earn cash commissions for specific business development activities, paid from the MSO:

### 6.1 School District Contracts
- **10% of district contract revenue for 2 years** from the contract start date
- Applies to any district Ariana personally sources and closes
- Commission tail terminates at end of year 2 regardless of contract length

### 6.2 Regional Center Contracts
- **10% of Regional Center revenue for 2 years** from the contract start date
- Same terms as district contracts

### 6.3 Corporate ABA Partnerships
- **15% one-time payment of first-year contract value**
- For partnerships with established ABA companies (e.g., Learn Behavioral, BlueSprig)
- Paid within 30 days of contract signing

### 6.4 Marketplace Provider Recruits
- **5% of marketplace fees for 12 months** generated by providers Ariana personally recruits
- Must be active recruitment (direct introduction, not passive referral)

### 6.5 Investor Sourcing
- **3% of invested capital** as a one-time finder's fee, capped at $50,000 per round
- Paid from the MSO after the round closes
- Applies only to investors Ariana actively sources and introduces

### 6.6 Attribution Rules
To earn commission, Ariana must:
- (a) Initiate the contact with the lead
- (b) Be present at the first substantive meeting
- (c) Remain actively involved through deal close
- (d) Have the attribution documented in writing (email, CRM entry) within 30 days of introduction

Disputes default to **no commission** unless Ariana can document her involvement.

### 6.7 Investment Decisions Remain with Jorrel
Ariana may source investment opportunities, but all decisions regarding whether to accept terms, valuation, and closing remain exclusively with Jorrel as Managing Member of the MSO. No commission is earned on declined deals.

## 7. Governance and Control

### 7.1 Jorrel as Managing Member
Jorrel serves as Managing Member of the MSO with unilateral authority over:
- Product, technology, and engineering decisions
- Marketing strategy and spend
- Hiring, firing, and compensation of MSO employees
- Contracts and vendor relationships under $50,000
- Day-to-day operational decisions

### 7.2 Reserved Matters (Require Both Parties' Consent)
The following actions require written approval from both Jorrel and Ariana:
- Sale of the MSO, Clinical LLC, or substantially all assets of either
- Dissolution of the MSO or Clinical LLC
- Taking on debt exceeding $100,000
- Issuing new equity in the MSO to any third party
- Amendments to this agreement
- Entering a new line of business outside behavioral health support
- Changes to the MSA Management Fee structure

### 7.3 Ariana's Role
Ariana serves as Chief Clinical Officer of the Clinical LLC with full authority over:
- Clinical service delivery and quality
- Clinician hiring and supervision within the Clinical LLC
- Clinical training and continuing education content
- HIPAA compliance and patient records management

## 8. Protections and Restrictive Covenants

### 8.1 IP Assignment
On the Effective Date, all intellectual property created by either party prior to the signing of this agreement in connection with Modern Village shall be irrevocably assigned to the MSO, including but not limited to:
- All software, code, databases
- Brand elements, logos, trademarks
- Marketing materials and copy
- Customer data and CRM records
- Lead lists and scraper outputs
- Any documentation or written materials

### 8.2 Non-Solicit (2 years post-departure)
For 2 years after ceasing to Actively Contribute, neither party shall:
- Solicit Modern Village customers, clients, or subscribers
- Recruit Modern Village employees or contracted clinicians
- Use Modern Village CRM data, lead lists, or trade secrets for any purpose

### 8.3 Non-Compete (Limited, CA-enforceable)
Given California's strict non-compete laws, a traditional non-compete is not enforceable. Instead, both parties agree to:
- Not start a directly competing neurodivergent parenting platform using Modern Village IP for 2 years
- Not use confidential business information (CRM data, financial records, strategic plans) for any competing purpose indefinitely

### 8.4 Right of First Refusal
- **On MSO sale:** If Jorrel receives an offer to sell the MSO, Ariana has first right to match the offer within 30 days
- **On Clinical LLC sale:** If Ariana receives an offer to sell the Clinical LLC, Jorrel has first right to match within 30 days
- These ROFRs are subject to the Drag-Along rights in Section 8.5

### 8.5 Drag-Along Rights
If Jorrel receives a bona fide acquisition offer for the MSO exceeding $3,000,000, Ariana is contractually obligated to sell her Clinical LLC at the same terms to the same buyer. This prevents sale paralysis.

### 8.6 Tag-Along Rights
If Ariana receives a bona fide acquisition offer for the Clinical LLC, Jorrel has the right to force the buyer to also acquire the MSO at fair market value as a condition of closing.

### 8.7 Buy-Sell on Death or Disability
- **Death:** The surviving partner has the right to purchase the deceased partner's entity at a pre-agreed formula (book value + 3x trailing 12-month EBITDA) within 180 days
- **Funding:** Both parties agree to maintain cross-purchase life insurance policies sufficient to fund the buy-out (premiums paid by respective entities)
- **Disability:** Defined as inability to perform duties for 180+ consecutive days, triggers same buy-out rights as death

## 9. Restructuring for Institutional Investment

Any such restructuring shall not materially reduce either party's economic rights as defined in this term sheet.

## 11. Dispute Resolution

- **Informal resolution first:** Any dispute must first be discussed between the parties with a 30-day good-faith resolution period
- **Mediation:** If informal resolution fails, parties shall engage a mutually agreed mediator (non-binding) for 60 days
- **Binding arbitration:** If mediation fails, disputes are resolved by binding arbitration in Los Angeles County, California, under JAMS rules
- **Prevailing party:** The prevailing party in arbitration is entitled to reasonable attorney fees and costs
