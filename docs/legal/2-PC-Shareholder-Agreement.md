**A California Professional Corporation**

**Effective Date:** [DATE]
**State of Formation:** California
**Secretary of State File Number:** [CA FILE NUMBER]

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, Modern Village PC (the "Corporation") was incorporated as a California professional corporation under the Moscone-Knox Professional Corporation Act (Corporations Code § 13400 et seq.) by the filing of Articles of Incorporation with the California Secretary of State on [FILING DATE];

WHEREAS, Ariana Roberts ("Ariana") is the sole shareholder, sole director, and sole officer of the Corporation, and is a duly licensed Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA) in good standing in the State of California;

WHEREAS, the Corporation has been organized for the purpose of providing behavior-analytic professional services, including but not limited to Parent Consultation services billed under Current Procedural Terminology (CPT) code 97156;

WHEREAS, the Corporation has entered into a Management Services Agreement (the "MSA") with Modern Village Services LLC (the "MSO"), under which the MSO provides non-clinical administrative, technology, marketing, and business support services to the Corporation; and

WHEREAS, Ariana desires to establish the governance of the Corporation, the rights and obligations of its sole shareholder, and the restrictions on transfer of its shares, all in compliance with California law.

NOW, THEREFORE, Ariana, as sole shareholder of the Corporation, adopts this Shareholder Agreement:

---

## ARTICLE 4 — GOVERNANCE

**4.1 Sole Director.** Ariana Roberts serves as the sole director of the Corporation.

**4.2 Officers.** The initial officers of the Corporation are:

- **President:** Ariana Roberts
- **Chief Clinical Officer:** Ariana Roberts
- **Secretary:** Ariana Roberts
- **Chief Financial Officer:** Ariana Roberts

The sole director may appoint additional officers as needed, provided that officers who hold themselves out as rendering Professional Services must be Licensed Persons.

**4.3 Clinical Authority.** Consistent with Section 7.3 of the Term Sheet and California's prohibition on the corporate practice of medicine (as analogized to the practice of behavior analysis), the sole director, in her clinical capacity, shall have exclusive and ultimate authority over:

(a) clinical service delivery and quality;

(b) clinician hiring, credentialing, supervision, discipline, and termination within the Corporation;

(c) development and approval of clinical training and continuing education content;

(d) HIPAA compliance, patient records management, and the content of clinical documentation;

(e) treatment planning and clinical decision-making for each patient or client;

(f) selection of diagnostic tools, assessment protocols, and clinical standards of care; and

(g) responses to clinical complaints, incidents, and licensing-board matters.

**4.4 Non-Clinical Support from MSO.** Pursuant to the MSA, the MSO provides the Corporation with non-clinical administrative, technology, marketing, billing, compliance support, and business-development services. The MSO shall not direct, supervise, or influence the practice of behavior analysis or the exercise of independent clinical judgment by any clinician of the Corporation.

---

## ARTICLE 5 — PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AND BILLING

**5.1 Services Rendered.** The Corporation renders Professional Services exclusively through Licensed Persons, including Parent Consultation services billed under CPT 97156 and such other BCBA services as may lawfully be provided.

**5.2 Billing.** The Corporation shall bill payors, clients, and third parties for Professional Services rendered. Revenue from such billings shall be received by the Corporation, subject to the management fee obligations set forth in the MSA.

**5.3 Independent Clinical Judgment.** Each clinician employed or engaged by the Corporation shall at all times exercise independent professional judgment with respect to the care of patients and clients. No term of the MSA or any other agreement shall be interpreted to require a clinician to act contrary to the clinician's independent professional judgment or ethical obligations.

---

## ARTICLE 6 — COMPLIANCE WITH CORPORATE-PRACTICE RESTRICTIONS

**6.1 Ownership Limited to Licensed Persons.** All issued and outstanding Shares shall at all times be held only by Licensed Persons permitted to own shares in a California professional corporation rendering behavior-analytic services, as required by the Moscone-Knox Professional Corporation Act and applicable regulations.

**6.2 No Lay Ownership.** No natural person or entity that is not a Licensed Person shall own, control, or beneficially hold any interest in the Shares, the voting rights of the Shares, or the Corporation's Professional Services revenue beyond what is permitted under California law.

**6.3 No Fee-Splitting.** No portion of the Corporation's fees for Professional Services shall be shared with any person or entity other than as permitted by law. The parties intend that all payments to the MSO under the MSA represent fair market value for bona fide administrative services and shall be structured accordingly.

**6.4 Disqualified Shareholder.** If any Shareholder ceases to be a Licensed Person (by reason of license lapse, suspension, revocation, death, or otherwise), the Shares held by such Shareholder shall be promptly transferred, redeemed, or otherwise disposed of in compliance with the Moscone-Knox Professional Corporation Act, within the time period required by law (currently ninety (90) days under Corporations Code § 13407, subject to confirmation by counsel).

---

## ARTICLE 7 — RESTRICTIONS ON TRANSFER OF SHARES

**7.1 General Restriction.** No Shares may be sold, transferred, pledged, hypothecated, gifted, or otherwise disposed of except in strict compliance with this Article 7, the Buy-Sell Agreement dated as of the Effective Date, and applicable California law.

**7.2 Qualified Transferees.** Shares may be transferred only to:

(a) another Licensed Person duly qualified to own shares in a California professional corporation providing behavior-analytic services; or

(b) a trust or estate-planning vehicle that holds the Shares solely for the benefit of a Licensed Person and that does not confer voting or management control on any non-Licensed Person.

**7.3 Right of First Refusal.** Any proposed transfer of Shares is subject to the right of first refusal in favor of the Corporation and then Jorrel Patterson, as set forth in the Buy-Sell Agreement.

**7.4 Void Transfers.** Any purported transfer in violation of this Article 7 shall be null and void and shall not be recognized by the Corporation.

**7.5 Legend.** Each stock certificate (or electronic record of ownership) shall bear a legend referencing this Agreement, the Buy-Sell Agreement, and the Moscone-Knox Professional Corporation Act.

---

## ARTICLE 8 — DIVIDENDS AND DISTRIBUTIONS

**8.1 Discretion.** Dividends and distributions shall be declared and paid at the sole discretion of the sole director, subject to applicable law, the Corporation's cash needs, and the MSA payment obligations.

**8.2 MSA Priority.** Amounts owed to the MSO under the MSA shall be paid or reserved before any dividend or distribution is made to the Shareholder.

---

## ARTICLE 9 — DISSOLUTION

**9.1 Events of Dissolution.** The Corporation shall be dissolved upon the first to occur of:

(a) the written election of the Shareholder to dissolve the Corporation;

(b) the death, disqualification, or permanent disability of the Shareholder, subject to the Buy-Sell Agreement;

(c) entry of a decree of judicial dissolution; or

(d) any other event requiring dissolution under the Act.

**9.2 Winding Up.** Upon dissolution, the Corporation shall wind up its affairs in accordance with the Act and the Moscone-Knox Professional Corporation Act, with particular attention to transition of patient care and preservation of patient records.

**9.3 Coordination with Pooling Agreement.** Dissolution of the Corporation is a Reserved Matter under the Pooling Agreement and requires prior written consent of Jorrel Patterson to the extent required thereunder.

---

## ARTICLE 10 — AMENDMENTS

**10.1 Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by the Shareholder. For so long as there is only one Shareholder, the Shareholder may amend this Agreement, subject to (a) compliance with California professional corporation law and (b) the Reserved Matters provisions of the Pooling Agreement.

## ARTICLE 11 — MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

**11.2 Dispute Resolution.** Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be resolved as provided in the Pooling Agreement (informal resolution, mediation, and binding arbitration in Los Angeles County, California under JAMS rules).

**11.3 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with the Articles, the Bylaws, the MSA, the Pooling Agreement, the Buy-Sell Agreement, and any HIPAA Business Associate Agreement with the MSO, constitutes the entire agreement of the Shareholder with respect to the subject matter hereof.

**11.4 Severability.** If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force.
