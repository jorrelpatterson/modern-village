# Workforce HIPAA Training Log

**Basis:** 45 CFR §164.308(a)(5) — security awareness and training for all workforce members
with access to ePHI. **Status: TEMPLATE 2026-07-15 — no training completed yet.**

being granted access and **annually** thereafter. Low-cost options exist (~$15–30/person online
